[h1]COMRADE, NOT EVERY HELICOPTER NEEDS A CONCRETE AIRPORT![/h1]

Sometimes a helicopter only needs a clear patch of ground, a dirt access road and somebody willing to wave it down.

[b]Free Heliports[/b] adds two compact grass-and-dirt landing areas for remote sites, temporary projects and anywhere a full concrete heliport would be excessive.

[h1]Included Heliports[/h1]

[b]Free Heliport Parking[/b]
[list]
[*] Stores one helicopter.
[*] Supports light and heavy helicopters.
[*] Free and placed instantly.
[*] Requires no workers, materials, electricity, heating, water or sewage.
[/list]

[b]Free Cargo Heliport[/b]
[list]
[*] Handles the normal cargo-heliport cargo categories.
[*] Includes fuel storage for helicopter operations.
[*] Retains road access and three factory/storehouse connections.
[*] Free and placed instantly.
[*] Loads and unloads at only 10% of normal speed.
[/list]

The cargo heliport is deliberately slow. It is a useful emergency or remote logistics point, not a free replacement for a properly equipped permanent cargo terminal.

[h1]Installation[/h1]

[b]Free Heliports requires TesmioLoader.[/b]

Subscribing installs the building assets normally, but the plugin DLL must be copied into TesmioLoader.

1. Subscribe to this mod and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3773169177]TesmioLoader[/url].
2. Find:
   Steam\steamapps\workshop\content\784150\3779842468\plugins\
3. Copy:
   free_heliports.dll
4. Paste it into:
   Steam\steamapps\common\SovietRepublic\tesmioloader\build\plugins\
5. Launch the game using:
   Steam\steamapps\common\SovietRepublic\tesmioloader\build\tesmiolauncher.exe
6. Make sure [b]free_heliports.dll[/b] is enabled in the TesmioLoader launcher.

The plugin is standalone. [b]Helicopter Distribution Office is not required[/b], although the two mods can be used together.
